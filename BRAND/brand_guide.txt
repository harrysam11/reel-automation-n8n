# Finance Reel Factory — Brand Identity Guide

## 1. Visual Identity
- Layout: 1080 x 1920 (9:16 Vertical Video format for Reels / Shorts)
- Background Color: #F4EBDD (Warm cream / paper card finish)
- Primary Accent: #1A1A1A (Deep charcoal / black text for maximum readability)
- Bullish / Growth Accent: #16A34A (Green badge)
- Risk / Alert Accent: #DC2626 (Red badge)
- Card Containers: #FFFFFF with 12px rounded corners and subtle drop shadow

## 2. Mascot & Character Rules
- Host: Sunglasses Cat (`BRAND/main_cat.png`)
- Role: Savvy, pragmatic, no-nonsense financial guide.
- Recurring Host Rule: Main cat introduces the hook, anchors transitions, and closes with the CTA.
- Meme Expressions: Authentic cat reaction photos (shocked, smug, calculating, facepalm).
- NO AI morphing or distorted AI cat generation; preserve crisp cartoon/authentic mascot identity.

## 3. Typography & Language (Hinglish)
- Latin Script: Bold Sans (Inter, Montserrat, Arial Black) for numbers and English terms (SIP, FD, DICGC, Nifty 50, CAGR).
- Devanagari Script: Noto Sans Devanagari for spoken Hindi narration text.
- Hierarchy:
  - Big Hook Headline: 48-64pt Bold
  - Supporting Subhead / Question: 32-40pt Medium
  - Data / Table / Chart Numbers: 36-48pt Bold Monospace/Sans
  - Footnote / Disclaimer: 18-24pt Regular

## 4. Automation Boundary & Compliance
- Financial Fact-Checking: Human verified via official SEBI/RBI/AMFI sources.
- Disclaimer: Educational purposes only; not SEBI-registered investment advice.
