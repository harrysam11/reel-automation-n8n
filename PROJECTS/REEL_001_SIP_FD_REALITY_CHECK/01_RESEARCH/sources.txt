## 1. Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)
- Primary Authority: AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India)
- Reference: https://www.amfiindia.com/investor-corner/knowledge-center/what-is-sip.html
- Fact: Minimum entry is as low as ₹100–₹500/month depending on scheme.
- Return profile: Equity market linked. Historical 10-15 year CAGR in broad index funds has hovered between 11%–14%, but returns are NOT guaranteed and suffer drawdown in market corrections.
- Taxation: LTCG above ₹1.25 Lakh taxed at 12.5% (post Union Budget 2024 update). STCG at 20%.

## 2. Fixed Deposits (FD) & Inflation Risk
- Primary Authority: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
- Average Scheduled Commercial Bank rates: 6.50% - 7.25% p.a. for 1–3 year tenures.
- Taxation: Interest income added to total taxable income and taxed at marginal income tax slab. TDS deducted under Section 194A if interest exceeds ₹40,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens).
- Real Return Reality: If headline CPI inflation runs at 5.0%–5.5% and 30% tax bracket applies on a 7% FD, net post-tax return is ~4.9%, resulting in negative or flat real purchasing power.

## 3. DICGC Deposit Insurance Guarantee
- Primary Authority: Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (Subsidiary of RBI)
- Reference: https://www.dicgc.org.in
- Fact: Each depositor in an insured bank is insured up to a maximum of ₹5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakhs) for both principal and interest amount held by them in the same capacity and same right across all branches of that bank.

## 4. Preservation of 14 Investment Categories & Indicative Entry Capital
Verified against SEBI master circulars and exchange listing norms:
1. Mutual Funds: ₹100–₹500 (AMFI approved minimum micro-SIPs)
2. ETFs: ~₹20–₹500 (Market price of 1 unit on NSE/BSE)
3. Stocks: ₹10–₹5,000+ (Fractional shares not permitted in India, single share price)
4. PPF / EPF / SSY: ₹250–₹500/year (Post Office & Government small savings schemes)
5. NPS: ₹500–₹1,000 (PFRDA minimum annual contribution rules)
6. FDs / RDs: ₹100–₹1,000 (Scheduled Commercial Banks & Post Office)
7. Government Securities: ₹10,000 (RBI Retail Direct primary auctions / T-bills)
8. REITs / InvITs: ₹100–₹400 (SEBI lot size = 1 unit on stock exchanges)
9. SGB (Sovereign Gold Bonds): ~₹7,500–₹8,500+ (1 gram gold sovereign issue price)
10. Corporate Bonds / NCDs: ₹10,000–₹1,00,000 (SEBI reduced face value norms to ₹10,000 for debt listings)
11. P2P / Invoice Discounting: ₹5,000–₹50,000 (RBI NBFC-P2P norms)
12. Real Estate: ₹20 lakh–₹1 crore+ (Direct physical land/apartment acquisition)
13. PMS (Portfolio Management Services): ₹50 lakh (Statutory SEBI minimum per investor)
14. AIF (Alternative Investment Funds): ₹1 crore (Statutory SEBI minimum per investor)
