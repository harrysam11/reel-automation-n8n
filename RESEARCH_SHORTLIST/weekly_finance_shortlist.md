## Topic 1: "SIP vs Lump Sum in 2026: The Dangerous Myth"
- **Target Audience:** First-time salaried investors with Diwali bonus or surplus cash.
- **The Hook (0–5s):** "Bonus ka paisa aaya hai aur ek saath mutual fund me daalne ki soch rahe ho? Ruko! Yeh galti aapka 1 saal ka return kha sakti hai!"
- **The Core Math/Fact:**
  - When Nifty 50 PE ratio is high (>22-24), Lump Sum investments have historically faced high drawdown risks in the subsequent 6–12 months.
  - STP (Systematic Transfer Plan): Park lump sum in an Arbitrage / Liquid Fund (tax efficient) and transfer weekly/monthly into Equity.
- **Regulatory Authority:** AMFI Mutual Fund Risk-o-meter / SEBI guidelines.
- **Slide Count:** 8 slides (Hook, Myth, Historical Nifty PE Drawdowns, Liquid Fund STP Strategy, Decision Matrix).

## Topic 2: "FD vs Debt Mutual Funds: The Post-2023 Tax Showdown"
- **Target Audience:** Conservative investors and senior citizens seeking safe yields.
- **The Hook (0–5s):** "Budget 2023 ke baad kya Debt Mutual Funds bilkul bekaar ho gaye? Sach suniye jo koi bank manager nahi batayega!"
- **The Core Math/Fact:**
  - Finance Act 2023 removed indexation benefits on debt mutual funds (<35% equity). Both FD and Debt MFs are taxed at marginal slab rates.
  - **The Hidden Edge of Debt Funds:** FD taxes interest *accrued annually* even if not withdrawn (TDS under Sec 194A). Debt funds allow *tax deferral*—tax is paid only upon redemption, compounding untaxed capital!
- **Regulatory Authority:** Income Tax Act Section 194A & 50AA.
- **Slide Count:** 8 slides (Bank Manager vs Savvy Cat, Tax Comparison Table, TDS Trap, Tax Deferral Compounding Visual).

## Topic 3: "The Silent Pickpocket: Why Your Savings Account Loses Money Every Day"
- **Target Audience:** People hoarding ₹2 Lakh–₹10 Lakh in 2.7% savings accounts.
- **The Hook (0–5s):** "Aapke savings account me pada paisa har ghante kamzor ho raha hai! Calculate kiya hai kabhi?"
- **The Core Math/Fact:**
  - Standard Savings Bank interest: 2.70%–3.00% p.a.
  - Average Food & Urban CPI inflation: 5.5%–6.5%.
  - Real wealth loss: -3.0% net compounding decline annually.
  - Solution: Auto-sweep facility (Sweep-in FD) or overnight/liquid funds without locking liquidity.
- **Regulatory Authority:** RBI Consumer Protection & Scheduled Commercial Bank Savings Interest rates.
- **Slide Count:** 7 slides (Leaking Wallet visual, Savings vs Inflation graph, Auto-Sweep mechanism, 3-step action plan).

## Topic 5: "Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) vs Digital Gold vs Physical Jewellery"
- **Target Audience:** Festive buyers and traditional gold accumulators.
- **The Hook (0–5s):** "Sona khareed rahe ho? Jeweller ko 15-20% making charge dekar khud ka nuksan mat karao!"
- **The Core Math/Fact:**
  - Physical Jewellery: Making charges (10-25%) + GST 3% = 15-28% instant haircut on day 1.
  - Digital Gold apps: 3% GST + high buy-sell spread (~3-5%), unregulated by SEBI/RBI.
  - Sovereign Gold Bonds (Secondary Market on NSE/BSE): Sovereign guarantee, zero making charge, 2.5% annual interest payout, and capital gains tax-exempt on maturity!
- **Regulatory Authority:** RBI SGB Scheme / SEBI Gold ETF regulations.
- **Slide Count:** 10 slides (Jewellery vs Digital vs SGB comparison matrix, Tax comparison, How to buy SGB on demat).
